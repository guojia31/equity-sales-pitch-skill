# Securities Compliance and De-Sensitization Rules

## Purpose

These rules govern the conversion of meeting notes and research materials into client-facing institutional sales pitches.

> When the disclosure status of material information cannot be confirmed, omit or generalize it. Do not convert potentially material non-public information into a public-facing investment conclusion.

This skill provides a drafting filter and does not replace the securities firm’s formal compliance review.

## 1. Public-Information Boundary

Information may be used as a client-facing fact only when it can reasonably be identified as public, including exchange filings, company announcements, financial reports, public investor-relations records, public performance briefings, official company channels, and government or industry-association publications.

Treat private calls, closed-door meetings, unpublished management commentary, unnamed channel checks, notes without a public source, and information described as pending disclosure as non-public unless public disclosure can be confirmed.

## 2. Source Hierarchy and Conflict Resolution

Use sources in this order: exchange or CNINFO filings; company financial reports, announcements and public investor-relations records; official company, government or industry-association publications; other primary public sources; reputable secondary sources.

- Do not treat another broker's report as final proof of an exact company fact when a primary disclosure exists.
- Do not trust a professionally formatted cover, table or chart without checking the underlying disclosure.
- Cross-check company name, security code, reporting period, unit, accounting scope and metric definition.
- If the supplied material conflicts with a higher-priority public source, use the higher-priority source and notify the user of the discrepancy.
- If one document contradicts itself, verify against the original filing. If the conflict remains unresolved, omit the figure.
- Never select the more optimistic number merely because it supports a stronger pitch.

## 3. Material Non-Public Information

Remove potentially material non-public information, including:

- Exact unannounced order values or quantities.
- Pending contract-signing or announcement dates.
- Undisclosed customer identities.
- Customer-specific prices, margins, volumes, inventories, or delivery schedules.
- Undisclosed capacity additions or commissioning dates.
- Undisclosed quarterly revenue, profit, margin, or shipment data.
- Internal budgets, forecasts, profit targets, or valuation expectations.
- Unannounced cooperation, acquisitions, financing, or equity incentives.

Do not retain sensitive information merely by adding `据调研` or `据了解`.

## 4. Customer and Order De-Sensitization

When the business logic remains valid after removing sensitive details, use descriptions such as `海外头部客户`、`国内头部客户`、`下游核心客户`、`头部电池企业`、`北美云厂商供应链` or `重点客户项目`.

Do not use generalized wording to conceal and republish an exact sensitive transaction. If an order itself has not been publicly disclosed and its existence is material, omit it entirely.

Unsafe:

> 公司将于8月披露某韩国客户12亿元订单，一期金额2亿元。

Safer:

> 海外客户拓展持续推进，相关项目转化将支撑海外业务占比提升，后续关注公开订单及交付进展。

## 5. Actual Results, Guidance, and Estimates

Always distinguish actual results, company guidance, analyst estimates, and scenarios.

- Disclosed historical results may use factual language.
- Guidance must use `公司预计`、`公司规划`、`公司目标` or `根据公司公开指引`.
- Estimates must use `预计`、`测算`、`对应` or scenario language.
- Scenario calculations must state material assumptions and verification conditions.

Never rewrite company guidance as completed or guaranteed performance.

Analyst forecasts must never be described as company guidance. Internal estimates and scenario calculations must not be presented as consensus forecasts unless a verifiable consensus source is available.

## 6. Forecast Language

Permitted restrained expressions include `预计`、`公司规划`、`公司目标`、`有望形成`、`具备提升基础`、`构成潜在增量`、`后续关注`、`取决于` and `仍需验证`.

Avoid `必然`、`板上钉钉`、`确定性极强`、`毫无风险`、`百分之百`、`稳赚`、`必将翻倍`、`无可替代` and `绝对垄断`.

Claims such as `不可替代`、`唯一供应商`、`全球第一` or `垄断` require public, authoritative, and clearly defined evidence.

## 7. Stock Price and Valuation

Do not predict a stock price, percentage increase, or investment return. Do not use `翻倍空间`、`三倍行情` or undisclosed target-market-value statements.

A valuation conclusion may be included only when the user supplies a compliant, publicly released research basis and explicitly requests it. Otherwise focus on operating drivers and verification indicators.

## 8. Capacity and Production Plans

Use exact capacity, commissioning, and utilization figures only when publicly disclosed. Otherwise:

- Use restrained general wording only when the project itself is public.
- Remove the project if its existence is non-public.
- Do not infer annual output directly from nameplate capacity.
- Distinguish capacity, production, shipment, and sales.

Do not mix gross capacity with effective capacity, or current capacity with planned capacity. State the expected commissioning date only when publicly disclosed, and do not imply immediate full utilization after commissioning.

## 9. Pricing, Profitability, and Earnings Quality

Do not disclose non-public customer prices, discounts, rebates, or contract margins. Public prices require clear source, specification, period, and unit.

Distinguish gross margin from net margin. Do not treat incremental gross profit as net profit or multiply nameplate capacity by spot price and call the result guaranteed profit. State assumptions and consider utilization, yield, depreciation, taxes, product mix, and qualification where material.

When profit growth is central to the pitch, check whether it includes material investment income, fair-value gains, government grants, impairment reversals, asset disposals or other non-recurring items. Do not attribute total net-profit growth to core operations when these items are material. Prefer operating indicators such as revenue, product gross margin, operating profit and shipment changes to support claims about core-business improvement.

Do not mix annual, quarterly and half-year figures without clear labels. Do not mix consolidated results with parent-company results, attributable net profit with total net profit, or audited results with unaudited estimates.

## 10. Rumours and Third-Party Claims

Do not repeat rumours, hearsay, or unverifiable channel checks as facts. For litigation, patents, policy, customer switching, or competitor exits, rely on public legal documents, official statements, or authoritative public sources.

Do not conclude that competitors will lose all market access or that the company will automatically obtain displaced share.

## 11. Recommendation Language

Permitted:

> 公司核心业务盈利改善，新产品放量构成新增量，后续关注产能爬坡与客户验证，建议重点关注。

Not permitted:

> 公司业绩确定爆发，当前是绝佳买点，股价至少翻倍。

The final sentence must remain `建议重点关注。` and must not be accompanied by a return guarantee.

## 12. Deletion Priority

When shortening a pitch, delete in this order:

1. Repeated facts.
2. Marginal businesses.
3. Distant optionality.
4. Non-material operating details.
5. Customer lists.
6. Unverified estimates.
7. Sensitive orders and transaction details.

Do not shorten the essential explanation of the demand driver, company position, financial transmission, or key verification indicator.

## 13. Mandatory Pre-Distribution Check

Before finalizing, confirm:

- Every exact number is public or clearly labeled as an estimate.
- Company name and security code match the original filing.
- Reporting period, unit, accounting scope and metric definition are consistent.
- Capacity, output, shipment, sales and order value have not been confused.
- Named customer relationships are publicly disclosed.
- No pending order, contract, announcement, or delivery information remains.
- Management guidance is clearly attributed.
- Scenarios are separated from actual results.
- Analyst forecasts are not presented as company guidance or realized results.
- Material non-recurring gains and losses have been checked before attributing profit growth to core operations.
- Any conflict between the supplied material and primary public disclosures has been resolved or omitted.
- No guaranteed performance or return is implied.
- No stock-price or market-value prediction appears.
- Every retained claim can be defended using a public source.
- Peripheral information has been deleted without damaging the causal chain.
- The text ends with `建议重点关注。`

If any item remains uncertain, remove or weaken the relevant statement before distribution.
